About

Why Augmentic Accounting exists.

Augmentic helps service-business accounting departments move from AI curiosity to governed, useful workflows that improve speed without weakening human review.

Built by

Bennett Bernard, CPA

More than a decade in accounting, paired with hands-on AI workflow design, automation, and finance operations experience.

Augmentic Accounting is built around a simple premise: AI will only create durable value in finance when it is tied to real workflows, clear review standards, and controls that accounting leaders can explain.

Written by Bennett Bernard, a CPA with over a decade of experience in the accounting profession, the site bridges hands-on accounting work with practical AI implementation.

The goal is to help service-business accounting teams identify the workflows worth changing, install the right operating structure, and keep adoption disciplined as the tools evolve.
