About

I’m an accountant who believes accountants should help build the future of the work.

I’m Bennett Bernard, a CPA with more than a decade in accounting and a hands-on obsession with AI, automation, and better ways of working.

The short version

Finance + code + practical curiosity.

I test new tools, build workflows, and share what I learn so more accountants can participate in the change instead of watching it happen to them.

Why Augmentic Accounting exists

AI coverage tends to land at one of two extremes: everything is about to change overnight, or none of it can be trusted. Neither view is very useful when you are responsible for real accounting work.

Augmentic Accounting is an applied AI lab for accounting and finance leaders who want to understand how AI works under the hood, build the infrastructure their teams need, and produce meaningful results beyond generic prompting.

I test what these tools can genuinely do, where they break, and how leaders can use them responsibly while keeping judgment and accountability human.

I share that work through the newsletter, YouTube walkthroughs, articles, practical products, webinars, and 1:1 coaching.
