How it works

Focused advice plus hands-on build support.

Use coaching when you need judgment and direction. Use implementation support when you want help turning the plan into workflows, SOPs, prompts, and controls.

Where Augmentic helps

The articles and resources are there for accountants who want to learn on their own. Dedicated support is for teams that want direct help applying the ideas.

Coaching focuses on judgment: where AI fits, what to avoid, how to review output, and how to talk about risk with leadership. Implementation focuses on buildout: prompts, SOPs, workflow specs, training, and control documentation.

The goal is practical adoption that improves speed without turning the accounting function into an uncontrolled experiment.
