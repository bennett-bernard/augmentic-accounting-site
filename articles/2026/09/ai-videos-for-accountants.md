## The workflow behind the videos

### 1. Choose the information

Start with a report and a clear idea of what the viewer should understand. For these examples, I used a sample tax return and a fictional company's profit and loss statement.

### 2. Build the video

Describe the story, scenes, and visual style. In the walkthrough, I use Codex and ChatGPT with Remotion to turn those instructions into video, then work through the results.

### 3. Review before sharing

Check the numbers, wording, timing, and branding. A polished animation still needs an accountant's judgment. I also talk through client data considerations and how skills could make the process repeatable.
